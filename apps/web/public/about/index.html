About Decision FC

decisionfc.com · a football career you play in five minutes

Decision FC is a football career simulator you play entirely through decisions. You are the player, not the manager. You start at sixteen, pick an academy, fight your way into the side, go out on loan, and then choose — season after season — the move that makes you or the move that pays you, the country you represent, the contract you sign, and the year you finally stop. Twenty seasons take about five minutes.

There is no match engine to watch and no team to pick. A card arrives, you weigh what it offers against what it costs, and the season plays out around that choice. It is a game about the decisions a career is actually made of.

What is in the world

  • 191 real clubs in twelve countries — England, Spain, Italy, Germany and France at two levels each, plus the top flights of Portugal, the Netherlands and Belgium. Saudi Arabia, the USA, Japan and China appear late in a career, where a move for money actually belongs.
  • A squad ladder you climb on merit. Six roles, from fringe player to the side being built around you, decided by your ability against that club's standard rather than by a dice roll — and the game shows you how far off the next rung you are.
  • A transfer market that behaves like one. Clubs sign players they can reach, loans stay inside the European pyramid, and every window reads as one coherent set of clubs rather than a random draw.
  • Three world leaderboards — achievements, career earnings and highest transfer value — plus a daily challenge that gives everybody the same football world for a day.

Is it a game of luck?

No, and that is measured rather than asserted. Playing well returns a median career score of 1991 against 1585 for playing at random, and wins on 70% of identical starting positions. Roughly 30% of the outcome is inside your control — which, for a game that also contains injuries, form and relegation, is the balance it is aiming for. Every probability the game shows you is the real one; that is checked automatically before each release.

Where the football comes from

Club divisions, reputations, academy strength and manager styles describe the real 2026/27 opening position, checked club by club against the season's own reporting. Player names, club names and competition names are used to describe real football; Decision FC is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or licensed by any club, league or competition.

Who makes it

Decision FC is made by one person, Davies Luo, and is not backed by a studio or a publisher. Behind the cards sit real transfer economics, tactical fit, hidden potential and a promotion and relegation pyramid.

Bugs, ideas and complaints are genuinely welcome: daviesluo@gmail.com. See the contact page.

How it pays for itself

The game is free, has no account, no purchases and no paid tier. It is funded by a single optional rewarded advert when you choose to play again — and the first replay of every day skips even that. Nothing ever interrupts a career in progress, and there are no adverts inside the game itself. What data that involves is set out in the privacy policy.

Where to play

In any browser, on a phone, tablet or computer, at decisionfc.com. Nothing to install; you can add it to your home screen if you want it to behave like an app. It is available in English and Simplified Chinese — the Chinese version is a rewrite rather than a translation.

关于 Decision FC

decisionfc.com · 五分钟走完一段足球生涯

Decision FC（足球生涯）是一款完全用「选择」来玩的足球生涯模拟游戏。 你扮演的是球员，不是教练。十六岁开始，选一家青训，一场一场把自己踢进一线队，出去租借， 然后一个赛季接一个赛季地做决定——是为了踢球转会还是为了钱，代表哪个国家，签哪一份合同， 什么时候挂靴。二十个赛季大约五分钟。

没有比赛要看，也没有阵容要排。一张卡片摆到你面前，你衡量它给你什么、又要你付出什么， 赛季就围着这个选择走下去。它讲的是一段生涯真正由什么构成。

这个世界里有什么

  • 十二个国家、191 家真实俱乐部——英西意德法各两个级别，加上葡超、荷甲、比甲。 沙特、美国、日本和中国联赛会在生涯后期出现，那正是「为钱转会」真正该发生的时候。
  • 队内地位要自己挣。六档定位，从边缘球员到全队围着你建， 由你的能力对上那家俱乐部的标准来决定，而不是掷骰子——而且游戏会告诉你，离上一档还差多少。
  • 一个像模像样的转会市场。球队只签他们够得着的人，租借只在欧洲体系内进行， 每个转会窗口摆出来的都是一组说得通的球队，而不是随机抽的。
  • 三个世界排行榜——成就、生涯总收入、最高身价； 外加每日挑战，全世界当天面对的是同一片足球世界。

是不是纯看运气

不是，而且这是测出来的、不是嘴上说的。会玩的打法中位生涯分是 1991，乱选是 1585； 在完全相同的开局下，会玩的赢 70%。大约 30% 的结果掌握在你手里—— 对一个同时包含伤病、状态和降级的生涯游戏来说，这正是它想要的平衡。 游戏里显示给你的每一个概率都是真实概率，这一点每次发版前都会自动核对。

足球数据从哪来

俱乐部所在级别、声望、青训强度和主教练打法，描述的是 2026/27 赛季开赛时的真实状况， 逐队对着当季报道核过。球员名、俱乐部名和赛事名用于描述真实足球； Decision FC 与任何俱乐部、联赛或赛事均无隶属、背书或授权关系。
